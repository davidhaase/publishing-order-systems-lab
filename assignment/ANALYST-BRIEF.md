# Analyst Brief

The company intends to replace the legacy wholesale order-processing capability.

Your task is to analyze the supplied implementation artifacts and create documentation using your own documentation strategy.

You may investigate the artifacts in any order.

## Known stakeholders
- Customer Service
- Sales Operations
- Distribution
- Finance
- EDI Operations
- Application Support
- Engineering

## Constraints
- Major trading partners must not experience an avoidable interruption.
- Historical order information must remain discoverable after migration.
- The legacy system contains behavior whose current business value has not been established.
- The target platform will not necessarily reproduce the legacy implementation architecture.

## Deliverable
Determine and document the product, its relevant implementation behavior, and the migration effort. Identify unresolved questions rather than inventing answers.
