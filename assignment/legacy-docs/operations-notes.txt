WHOLESALE ORDER SUPPORT NOTES
Last revised: 2021-06-17

1. EDI orders normally arrive overnight. Large partners sometimes send multiple
   batches during holiday periods.

2. If an order is missing, search by customer PO first. Do not ask the partner
   for the internal order number.

3. BookMart is special. Do not release a BookMart order manually because the
   account has its own agreement. Ask Sales Ops if there is a pricing question.

4. "BO" usually means backorder. Rush orders should not sit in BO status.

5. The OLDPO report prints after nightly processing. Finance used to collect it
   from the printer. Unsure whether anybody still uses it.

6. If an ISBN fails, verify what the partner actually sent before changing the
   title master.

7. READMORE prices have occasionally been strange. Customer Service normally
   checks the PO and calls Sales Ops.

8. EDI acknowledgments are expected by partners. There was once a spreadsheet
   of partner-specific acknowledgment rules; location unknown.
