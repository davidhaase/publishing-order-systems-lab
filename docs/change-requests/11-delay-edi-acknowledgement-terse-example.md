# Change Specification: Delay EDI Acknowledgement - Terse Example

## Status

Draft — generated for human review.

## Business Objective

Ensure the 855 is sent with the order's final price rather than before pricing is complete.

## Current Behavior

The 855 is being sent before order pricing is complete.

## Desired Behavior

Defer sending the 855 until the order has been successfully priced and the final price is available.

## Trigger

Successful completion of order pricing with a final price available.

## Business Rules

1. An 855 must not be sent before the order has been successfully priced and a final price is available.
2. When pricing succeeds and the final price is available, the 855 should be sent.

## Exceptions

1. Pricing fails, is held, or remains pending; the required 855 behavior for these states has not been decided.

## Affected Systems and Interfaces

1. The system or process that generates and sends the 855 (not yet identified).
2. The order-pricing process (specific system not yet identified).
3. The 855 recipient/interface (not yet identified).

## Acceptance Criteria

1. While an order is still being priced, no 855 is sent for that order.
2. After an order is successfully priced and its final price is available, the 855 is sent.

## Assumptions

None identified.

## Open Questions

1. Operations must decide what should happen to the 855 when pricing fails, is held, or remains pending.
2. Which system or process sends the 855, and who receives it?
