# Change Specification: Delay EDI Acknowledgement - Uncertain example

## Status

Draft — generated for human review.

## Business Objective

Prevent customers from receiving EDI 855 acknowledgements before the order has a final price.

## Current Behavior

For EDI orders, the outbound 855 acknowledgement is sent too early—before the order is priced. The requester believes, but has not confirmed, that the 855 is created immediately after the inbound 850 is accepted.

## Desired Behavior

For an EDI 850 order, send the outbound 855 only after pricing completes successfully and the order has a final price.

## Trigger

Successful completion of pricing with a final order price for an EDI 850 order.

## Business Rules

1. The outbound EDI 855 for an EDI 850 order must wait until pricing has completed successfully and the order has a final price.

## Exceptions

1. Pricing failure, pricing hold, and pricing remaining pending are identified conditions, but the required 855 behavior for each has not been decided.

## Affected Systems and Interfaces

1. Inbound EDI 850 order processing
2. Outbound EDI 855 acknowledgement processing
3. Order pricing process

## Acceptance Criteria

1. For an EDI 850 order whose pricing completes successfully and produces a final price, the outbound 855 is generated or sent only after that successful pricing completion.

## Assumptions

1. The system or process that generates and sends the outbound 855 has not yet been identified.

## Open Questions

1. What should happen to the 855 when pricing fails?
2. What should happen to the 855 when pricing is held?
3. What should happen to the 855 when pricing remains pending, including whether any timeout applies?
4. Which application, interface, or process currently creates and sends the outbound 855?
