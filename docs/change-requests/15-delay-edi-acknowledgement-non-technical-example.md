# Change Specification: Delay EDI Acknowledgement — Non-technical example

## Status

Draft — generated for human review.

## Business Objective

Prevent customers receiving an EDI order confirmation before the final amount they will pay has been determined.

## Current Behavior

For EDI orders, the 855 is believed to be created immediately after the inbound 850 is accepted, before the order is priced.

## Desired Behavior

For EDI orders with successful pricing, send the 855 once the order has a final price.

## Trigger

Successful completion of pricing for an EDI order, with the final price available.

## Business Rules

1. An EDI 855 may be sent when pricing has completed successfully and the final price is available.
2. The 855 must not be sent before the order has been priced.

## Exceptions

1. Pricing failure, pricing hold, or another non-success pricing outcome: required 855 behavior has not been decided.

## Affected Systems and Interfaces

1. EDI inbound 850 processing
2. Order pricing process
3. EDI outbound 855 generation/transmission

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Given an EDI order whose pricing completes successfully and produces a final price, the system sends an 855 after that successful pricing completion.
2. Given an EDI order that has not yet completed pricing successfully, the system does not send an 855.

## Assumptions

1. The specific application or integration that generates and transmits the 855 has not yet been identified.

## Open Questions

1. What should happen to the 855 when pricing fails, is held, remains pending, or completes with another non-success outcome? Operations needs to decide.
2. Which application or integration currently creates and sends the 855?
