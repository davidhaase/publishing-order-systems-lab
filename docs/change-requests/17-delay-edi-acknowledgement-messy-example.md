# Change Specification: Delay EDI Acknowledgement - Messy Example

## Status

Draft — generated for human review.

## Business Objective

Prevent EDI 855 acknowledgements from being sent before an order has been priced and a final price is available.

## Current Behavior

EDI 855 acknowledgements are being sent too early. It is believed, but not confirmed, that the 855 is created immediately after 850 acceptance.

## Desired Behavior

For an EDI order whose pricing succeeds, create and send the 855 only after the order has a final price.

## Trigger

Successful pricing of an EDI order, with the final price available, permits creation and sending of the 855.

## Business Rules

1. The change applies only to EDI orders.
2. For an EDI order with successful pricing, the 855 must wait until the final price is available.

## Exceptions

1. Pricing failure requires special handling, but the required 855 behavior has not yet been decided by Operations.
2. Behavior for pricing outcomes other than successful pricing, including pending, held, delayed, or otherwise incomplete pricing, has not been established.

## Affected Systems and Interfaces

1. EDI order processing and the EDI 855 acknowledgement flow.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Given an EDI order whose pricing completes successfully and produces a final price, the system creates and sends the 855 only after that successful pricing outcome and final price are available.

## Assumptions

1. The current 855 creation event is believed to be immediately after 850 acceptance; this needs confirmation and is not a confirmed current-state fact.

## Open Questions

1. Operations: What should happen to the 855 when pricing fails?
2. Operations: Which other pricing outcomes, if any, should permit, suppress, delay, or otherwise affect 855 creation?
3. What component or process currently creates the 855, and is 850 acceptance actually its current trigger?
