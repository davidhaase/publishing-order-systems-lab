# Change Specification: Delay EDI Acknowledgement - full details one shot

## Status

Draft — generated for human review.

## Business Objective

Prevent customers from receiving an EDI 855 acknowledgement before the final price for an EDI 850 order is known.

## Current Behavior

The requester believes the EDI 855 is currently sent immediately after EDI 850 acceptance; Engineering must verify the actual outbound-855 trigger and involved components.

## Desired Behavior

For EDI 850 orders, do not send an EDI 855 until pricing has succeeded and a final price is available; then send the 855.

## Trigger

Pricing for an EDI 850 order has succeeded and a final price is available.

## Business Rules

1. An EDI 855 for an EDI 850 order may be sent only after pricing has succeeded and a final price is available.

## Exceptions

1. Pricing failure: required EDI 855 behavior is undecided.
2. Pricing hold: required EDI 855 behavior is undecided.

## Affected Systems and Interfaces

1. EDI 850 order processing.
2. Outbound EDI 855 acknowledgement process.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Given an EDI 850 order whose pricing is still in progress, no EDI 855 is sent.
2. Given an EDI 850 order for which pricing has succeeded and a final price is available, an EDI 855 is sent.
3. For successfully priced EDI 850 orders, an EDI 855 is not sent before final pricing is available.

## Assumptions

1. The requested timing change applies to outbound EDI 855 acknowledgements associated with EDI 850 orders.
2. Engineering will identify and validate the applications and integration components that determine when the EDI 855 is sent.
