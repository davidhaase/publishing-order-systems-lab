# Change Specification: [Change Request]: Delay EDI acknowledgement

## Status

Draft — generated for human review.

## Business Objective

Prevent partners from receiving incomplete acknowledgements when an order price is not yet known.

## Current Behavior

Partners currently receive acknowledgements before the order price is known, resulting in incomplete acknowledgements.

## Desired Behavior

Delay partner acknowledgements until the order price is known.

## Trigger

The order price becomes known/available.

## Business Rules

1. A partner acknowledgement must not be sent while the order price is unknown.

## Exceptions

1. Pricing may fail, be held, or remain pending; the required acknowledgement behavior for these states is not yet defined.

## Affected Systems and Interfaces

None identified.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. When an order price is unknown, no acknowledgement is sent to the partner.

## Assumptions

None identified.

## Open Questions

1. Which system or interface sends the acknowledgement, and which partners and order types are in scope?
2. When a price becomes available, should the acknowledgement be sent automatically, and is the price required in the acknowledgement?
3. What acknowledgement behavior is required when pricing fails, is held, or remains pending?
