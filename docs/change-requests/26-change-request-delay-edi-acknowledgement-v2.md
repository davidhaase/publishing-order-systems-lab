# Change Specification: [Change Request]: Delay EDI Acknowledgement v2

## Status

Draft — generated for human review.

## Business Objective

Avoid partner confusion caused by EDI 855 acknowledgements that do not include a known final order price.

## Current Behavior

For EDI orders, the 855 is believed to be created and sent too early—possibly immediately after the 850 is accepted—before the order has been priced.

## Desired Behavior

For an EDI order whose pricing succeeds, send the 855 after the order has been priced and the final price is available.

## Trigger

Successful completion of pricing for an EDI order, with a final price available.

## Business Rules

1. The change applies to EDI orders and their 855 acknowledgements.
2. An 855 for a successfully priced EDI order must not be sent before the final order price is available.
3. Once pricing succeeds and the final price is available, the 855 should be sent.

## Exceptions

1. Pricing cannot complete or fails for an EDI order; required 855 behavior is not yet defined.
2. An EDI order is held or otherwise remains pending pricing; required 855 behavior is not yet defined.

## Affected Systems and Interfaces

1. EDI 850 order intake flow.
2. EDI 855 acknowledgement generation/transmission flow.
3. Order-pricing process or service.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Given an EDI order is still awaiting pricing, the system does not send its 855 acknowledgement.
2. Given pricing for an EDI order completes successfully and a final price is available, the system sends the 855 acknowledgement after that condition is met.

## Assumptions

1. The requester's statement that the 855 is created immediately after 850 acceptance is tentative and requires validation during implementation analysis.

## Open Questions

1. Operations must define the required 855 handling when pricing fails or cannot complete.
2. Operations must define the required 855 handling while an order is held or pending pricing.
3. Which application or component generates and transmits the 855 should be identified for implementation scope.
