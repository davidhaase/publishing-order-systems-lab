# Change Specification: [Change Request]: Delay acknowledgment v3

## Status

Draft — generated for human review.

## Business Objective

Prevent partners from receiving incomplete acknowledgements before an order price is established.

## Current Behavior

The EDI system sends partner acknowledgements before the price is established.

## Desired Behavior

The EDI system must delay partner acknowledgements until the price is established.

## Trigger

A price is established for the order.

## Business Rules

1. Partner acknowledgements must not be sent before the price is established.

## Exceptions

1. Pricing may fail, remain pending, or be placed on hold; the required acknowledgement behavior for these states is not yet defined.

## Affected Systems and Interfaces

1. EDI system
2. Partner acknowledgement interface

## Acceptance Criteria

1. When an order does not yet have an established price, the EDI system does not send its partner acknowledgement.

## Assumptions

None identified.

## Open Questions

1. What acknowledgement behavior is required when pricing fails, remains pending, or the order is on hold?
