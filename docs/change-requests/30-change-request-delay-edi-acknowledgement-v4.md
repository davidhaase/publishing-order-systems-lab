# Change Specification: [Change Request]: Delay EDI acknowledgement v4

## Status

Draft — generated for human review.

## Business Objective

Prevent partners from receiving incomplete EDI 855 acknowledgments by ensuring the order price is final before the acknowledgment is sent.

## Current Behavior

For EDI orders, the 855 is believed to be created/sent shortly after the 850 is accepted, before the order has been priced to a final price.

## Desired Behavior

Delay the EDI 855 acknowledgment until the order has a final price, then send the 855 to the partner.

## Trigger

An EDI order's price becomes final.

## Business Rules

1. An EDI 855 must not be sent before the associated order has a final price.
2. When an EDI order has a final price, the 855 should be sent to the partner.

## Exceptions

1. The required 855 behavior when pricing fails is not yet decided.
2. The required 855 behavior while pricing is held or pending is not yet decided.

## Affected Systems and Interfaces

1. EDI order processing for inbound 850 orders and outbound 855 acknowledgments.
2. Order pricing process/status, insofar as it establishes that an order price is final.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Given an EDI order whose price is not final, the system does not send its 855 acknowledgment.
2. Given an EDI order whose price becomes final, the system sends its 855 acknowledgment to the partner.

## Assumptions

1. The existing 855 timing is believed, rather than confirmed, to occur shortly after the inbound 850 is accepted.

## Open Questions

1. Operations must decide the required 855 behavior when pricing fails.
2. Operations must decide the required 855 behavior while pricing is held or remains pending.
3. The current event and component that create or transmit the 855 should be validated.
