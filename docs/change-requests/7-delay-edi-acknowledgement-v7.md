# Change Specification: Delay EDI Acknowledgement v7

## Status

Draft — generated for human review.

## Business Objective

Prevent premature EDI 855 order acknowledgements so that acknowledgements are sent only after the order has been successfully priced and has a final price.

## Current Behavior

For EDI orders, the 855 is sent too early, before pricing has finished. The requester believes it may be created immediately after the 850 is accepted, but this specific current trigger is unconfirmed.

## Desired Behavior

For an EDI order, the system must wait to send the 855 until pricing has succeeded and a final price is available.

## Trigger

Successful completion of pricing, with a final order price available.

## Business Rules

1. An EDI 855 must not be sent while order pricing is incomplete.
2. An EDI 855 must be sent once pricing has succeeded and a final order price is available.

## Exceptions

1. Pricing fails, is held, or remains pending; the required 855 handling for these states has not been decided.

## Affected Systems and Interfaces

1. EDI order processing for inbound 850 orders and outbound 855 acknowledgements.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Given an EDI order whose pricing is still in progress, no 855 acknowledgement is sent.
2. Given an EDI order whose pricing has completed successfully and has a final price, an 855 acknowledgement is sent after that successful pricing state is reached.

## Assumptions

1. The requester’s statement that the 855 may currently be created immediately after 850 acceptance is tentative and requires confirmation during implementation analysis.
2. The specific application or EDI component that creates and sends the 855 is not yet identified.

## Open Questions

1. Operations must decide how the 855 should be handled when pricing fails, is held, or remains pending.
2. Which application or EDI component currently creates and sends the 855?
