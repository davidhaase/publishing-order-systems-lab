# Change Specification: Delay EDI Acknowledgement v8

## Status

Draft — generated for human review.

## Business Objective

Prevent EDI 855 order acknowledgements from being sent before an order has been priced, so the acknowledgement can be sent with the final price after successful pricing.

## Current Behavior

For EDI orders, the outbound 855 is sent immediately and can be sent before pricing has finished. The requester believes it is created after the inbound 850 is accepted, but this triggering detail has not been confirmed.

## Desired Behavior

For an EDI order whose pricing succeeds, send the outbound 855 only once the order has a final price.

## Trigger

Successful completion of pricing for an EDI order, with a final price available.

## Business Rules

1. An outbound 855 for an EDI order must not be sent before successful pricing has completed and a final price is available.
2. When pricing succeeds and the final price is available, the 855 is to be sent.

## Exceptions

1. Pricing fails; the required 855 behavior has not been decided.
2. Pricing remains pending; the required 855 behavior has not been decided.
3. Pricing is held; the required 855 behavior has not been decided.

## Affected Systems and Interfaces

1. EDI order processing, including inbound 850 processing and outbound 855 acknowledgement processing.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Given an EDI order is still awaiting pricing, when pricing has not successfully completed with a final price, the system does not send its 855.
2. Given an EDI order completes pricing successfully and has a final price, the system sends its 855 after that successful pricing completion.

## Assumptions

None identified.

## Open Questions

1. Operations must decide whether, when, and with what content an 855 is sent when pricing fails, remains pending, or is held.
2. Which application, integration, or interface generates and sends the outbound 855?
3. Confirm whether the current 855 trigger is acceptance of the inbound 850.
